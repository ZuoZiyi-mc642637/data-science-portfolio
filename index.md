## Project Showcase

### Movie Scores Analysis: Do Critics and Audiences Agree?

**Research Question:**
Do Rotten Tomatoes critic scores (Tomatometer) and audience scores agree for popular films, and where do they diverge most?

**Visualization:**
![Movie Scores Comparison](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZuoZiyi-mc642637/data-science-project/main/movie_scores.png)

**Key Findings:**
Critics rated *The Shape of Water*, *Black Panther*, and *Dunkirk* higher than audiences did, while *The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey* shows the opposite pattern. Only *The Martian* received nearly identical scores from both groups. This suggests that critic–audience disagreement is common and direction varies by film.
